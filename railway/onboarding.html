Four steps to a protected meeting.

This checklist tracks your real progress — it checks itself off as you go. Nothing records until you deploy your first bot, and every free account includes 5 monitored hours a month.

1
Sign in with Google
Your workspace, captures, and detection history are tied to your Google account.
Sign in with Google
2
Deploy your first bot
Paste a Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams link in the console — the bot joins as a visible participant and every speaker gets a live verdict within seconds. Announce recording to participants; it's required.
Open the console
3
Set incident alerts
Add a Slack webhook in the console's Alerts card so a wire-hold incident pings your channel the moment it fires — even when nobody's watching the dashboard.
Add a webhook
4
Enroll a voiceprint (optional)
After your first meeting, enroll a speaker from their captures. Every later verdict then also checks "is this even their voice?" — a second factor on top of deepfake detection.
