STEP 2 OF 4

Admin consent

Sonave joins calls as a visible participant — everyone on the call sees it. Your Workspace admin enables third-party media access once, org-wide.

01
In the Admin console, open Apps → Google Workspace → Google Meet.
02
Enable "Let third-party apps that join a Meet call use the audio and video of the call."
03
Approve Sonave in the allowed-apps list. Changes can take up to 24 hours.
Not the admin?
🛡Participants are always notified when Sonave is on a call, and any of them can turn it off. Consent is a feature, not friction.
