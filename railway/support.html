Frequently Asked Questions

How do I launch Sonave inside Google Meet™?

Once installed from the Google Workspace Marketplace™, join any Google Meet™ call, click the Activities icon (triangle, square, circle) in the bottom-right bar, and select Sonave. The side panel will open and begin verifying speakers live in real time.

How do I unlock the 4 bonus free hours?

Every account includes 1 hour of live voice verification upon install. You can unlock 4 additional hours (5 total free hours) by leaving a quick 30-second review on our Google Workspace Marketplace™ listing. The bonus hours are automatically unlocked on your account.

Does Sonave store or record our meeting audio?

No. Sonave processes audio ephemerally in volatile RAM strictly for the continuous 4-second sliding evaluation window. Audio is never permanently written to disk or used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full compliance details.

How does the automated wire-hold trigger work?

If an active speaker is evaluated as a synthetic voice clone (FAKE) for three consecutive 4-second analysis windows, Sonave automatically raises a wire-hold incident and can dispatch an instant webhook to your treasury ERP (e.g. Coupa, SAP, or Kyriba) to halt payment approvals before funds clear.
